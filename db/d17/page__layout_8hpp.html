Detailed Description

The geometry of a document page, in millimetres, and the pure functions that lay it out.

A page is a sheet of fixed physical size; its regions are rectangles of it, pinned to its margin box or placed at an offset from its edges. Both frontends and the recording backend take a page's scale and its regions' rectangles from layoutPage, so they cannot disagree about where a footer is; a frontend adds only its density. See docs/spec/ui/document_page.md.
