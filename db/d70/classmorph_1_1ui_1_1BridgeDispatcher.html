Detailed Description

A Dispatcher over the bridge's model-free seams: bindByType, executeRaw and the string-keyed attach, through RawHandler.

An evaluation goes through the Evaluator it was given, since that envelope belongs to the UI service, not the bridge; without one it is refused with a std::runtime_error. It has no offline queue of its own: queueOffline is ignored unless a QueueingDispatcher wraps it. A shared binding without a key is bound unattached, which the bridge refuses at the first call; the interpreter never binds one, since a "shared" alias is a stateless model's and binds privately.
