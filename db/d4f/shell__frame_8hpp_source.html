42/// @brief What ends the line of a head's trail, which decides how far a compact top bar's trailing end stands from the
43/// window's edge: a button that draws no frame pads itself, so a frame leaves it to the button; anything else
44/// reaches the edge of its box.
45enum class HeadTrailEnd : std::uint8_t {
46 None, ///< Nothing is in the trail: the lead's text or badges end the line.
47 Unframed, ///< A ghost or icon-only action, or the head menu's trigger, which carries its own padding.
48 Framed, ///< A framed action, or a head tool such as a select.
49};

61/// @brief The frame around a client's screens.
62///
63/// A row filling the window: the sidebar (surface `sidebar`, `kShellSidebarWidth` units wide), holding the
64/// application's name and a `Navigator`, and the main column holding the top bar (surface `toolbar`) above the page
65/// (surface `page`), where the client places the workspace. The top bar is one line that wraps only when it is out of
66/// room: the shown screen's title with the active head's lead (subtitle and badges) under it, and the head's trail
67/// (tools and actions, then the principal while it sits in the head) at the trailing edge, dropping to a line of its
68/// own where it does not fit. The principal and a sign-out button sit at the sidebar's foot. Signed out, the sidebar
69/// and the top bar are hidden and the page is all there is. Compact (`setCompact(true)`), the sidebar is hidden and
70/// the top bar starts with a navigation menu over the navigator's entries, ending with the principal's sign-out entry,
71/// so the page has nearly the whole window. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its backend.
