Detailed Description

The catalog as a list a person opens screens from, with a title search and pinned shortcuts.

Entries are grouped by module, then group, in the order each first appears; each group has a heading. Each entry is a list item (ButtonStyle::Item) as wide as the navigator, and setCurrent marks the open screen's. An unavailable entry is listed disabled, with its reason as its tooltip. The search shows the entries one of whose titles contains the text, ignoring ASCII case, and the headings of the groups that still show one. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its backend.

Test ids: navigator, navigator_search, navigator_pins, nav_<screen id>, pin_<screen id>, and navigator_group_<module>/<group> for a heading.
