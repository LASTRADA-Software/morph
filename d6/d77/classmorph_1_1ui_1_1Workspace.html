Detailed Description

Tabs over screens: one screen instance per tab, mounted into the tab's page through DocumentScreenHost::mountInto.

Tabs keep the order they were opened in. Opening a screen whose identity an open tab already has focuses that tab; a singleton screen has one tab. Closing runs each screen's close protocol (spec 6 §3), one tab at a time, and a walk over several tabs stops at the first veto. The shell takes Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab and Ctrl+W, which documents may not declare. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its runtime.
