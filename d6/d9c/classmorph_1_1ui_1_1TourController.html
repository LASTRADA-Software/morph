Detailed Description

Runs at most one of the app's tours: opens each step's screen, prepares a shell target, points the spotlight at the step's target, and moves on with Next, Back, a waiting step's screen, or End.

A shell makes one with each frame and destroys it with the frame, which ends a running tour. It reads the tours from AppRoot::tours(), runtime tours included, and follows them while a tour runs, so a change of locale re-evaluates the card's texts. Neither copyable nor movable; on the runtime's owner.
