std::optional< Refusal > morph::ui::servedScreenRefusal ( std::string_view screenId)
inlinenodiscard

Why a server's catalog entry may not be mounted as a screen, when it may not.

The client's own documents (the chat panel, the tour card) carry host values and commands no other document may reach, so their ids are the client's and never a server's.

Parameters
screenIdThe catalog entry's id.
Returns
The refusal, naming screenId, when it is a reserved id; otherwise nothing.

Why a loaded server document may not be mounted under its catalog entry, when it may not.

Host names are scoped by the id a mounted document carries, so a document that names another id than its entry could take that id's scope.

Parameters
screenIdThe catalog entry's id.
documentIdThe id the loaded document names.
Returns
The refusal, naming screenId, when the two ids differ; otherwise nothing.
