Detailed Description

The framework's store: a map held in memory, on its own strand, each person seeing and changing only their own screens. It keys every entry by the person and reads nothing else of the session.

Its entries last as long as it does. Each call is a task on the strand, and its completion is delivered there. It bounds neither the number of entries nor their total size, so it serves tests and examples, not a deployment.
