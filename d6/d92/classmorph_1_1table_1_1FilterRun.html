Detailed Description

Evaluates a compiled filter over a candidate list, column at a time, in steps that yield at a deadline or a stop request.

Each column filter, then each group, compacts the surviving candidates in place, so later columns test fewer rows. The order of the candidates is kept, which is how a filter over a cached sorted order stays sorted.
