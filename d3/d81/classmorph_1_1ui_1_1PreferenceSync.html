Detailed Description

Talks to the server's preferences model (morph.preferences) for one application, through the interpreter's Dispatcher.

An application's InterpreterHost holds one, calls load after it mounts a screen, and forwards preferenceChanged to changed and resetPrefs to reset. No call names a person: the server keys every entry by the verified session's principal. All calls are made on the runtime's owner, where their replies arrive.

Loads screen's stored choices, in one call for all its points, and sets each that fits.

A point with no stored entry keeps the document's default. A stored entry that does not parse as the point's type, or that the point refuses (Screen::setPreference), is ignored, so a new document version never breaks on an old choice. A screen with no points makes no call and reports at once.

Parameters
screenThe mounted screen. Borrowed until the reply is delivered or scope stops.
scopeGates the delivery: stop or destroy it with the screen, and nothing touches the screen after.
doneCalled once with what was applied and ignored, or the reason the call failed; may be empty.
