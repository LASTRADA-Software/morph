Whether a session's screens record what the development inspector reads, and whether a frontend opens the inspector (spec 6 §21).

Enumerator

Nothing is recorded, no inspector is handed out, and the inspector's shortcut does nothing.

On 

Each screen records its lowered nodes, its live scopes and its latest command lists.
