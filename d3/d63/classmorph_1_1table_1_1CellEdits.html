Detailed Description

Commits cell edits row by row, in order.

Owner-affine. A completion that arrives after the CellEdits is destroyed is ignored, and destroying it ends the engine edit of every row it still has in flight. The commit function, the patcher and the engine's handlers may commit again, or destroy this object, from inside a call it makes.
