Detailed Description

Stores custom screens. The application's implementation is where every policy over them lives.

list answers the screens a person may open, which may include screens others saved. put and remove refuse what the person may not do, with a reason for the person. An id longer than kMaxNameBytes is shown to the person cut, so get and remove could not be given it back: a store keeps its ids within kMaxNameBytes (the model's own are 39 bytes). The model calls a store only with a person the server derived and with a document that passed every check. Every call carries the verified session too (CustomScreenKey::session, CustomScreenScope::session), so a policy may depend on more than the person, such as a role the session's token grants. A store owns the executor its completions are delivered on.

An entry list answers whose id lacks kCustomScreenIdPrefix is listed broken, and get and remove refuse its id before the store is called: the model cannot reach it, so cleaning it up is the store's.

The model's checks keep a person from saving a document that exceeds their permissions; they do not decide who may overwrite whose screen. That is the store's: see put.
