Detailed Description

What an action changes, and which records its result holds.

BRIDGE_REGISTER_EFFECTS records an action's effects as data under its id. Nothing is matched or checked at registration, so the order of record, action and effects registrations across translation units does not matter. The first read through effectsOf, recordsOf, recordNamed or actionsHolding matches every action's result slots against the record registry and validates the whole configuration, once. A configuration with problems throws morph::model::EffectsRegistrationError naming all of them, on that read and on every later one.

Declares what action A changes: any of morph::effects::updates<R>, changes<R>, changesInstance and none.

Place it beside the action's registration, where ActionTraits<A> is visible. It defines no specialisation, so a translation unit that does not see it is consistent with one that does. Repeating the same effects is a no-op; different effects for one action are refused on the first read. Expands to nothing under MORPH_CLIENT_ONLY.

...The effects.
