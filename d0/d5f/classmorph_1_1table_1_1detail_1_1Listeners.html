Detailed Description

A source's listeners, notified so that a listener may unsubscribe itself or another, or destroy the source, from inside a notification.

A listener unsubscribed during a notification is not called after that, even if it was registered when the notification began: unsubscribing is how its owner says it is about to be destroyed. A source destroyed during a notification ends it.
