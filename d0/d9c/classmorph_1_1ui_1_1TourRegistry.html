Detailed Description

The tours added while the server runs, beside the app document's own.

add and replace take one tour's JSON, the form of an entry of the app document's tours (author::tourJson writes it), and check it against the bundle served now exactly as the bundle's build checks the app document's tours, and that its t keys are in a catalogue of the bundle or among the server's texts. The bundle is read through the getter at every call; when its digest differs from the one last checked, every held tour is checked again first, and one that fails is dropped: logged at error and told to setOnDropped's handler. Every change, a drop included, is told to setOnChanged's handler after the registry's lock is released (publishTourChanges turns it into a change of the record morph.tours). The first change made while no handler is set is logged as a warning, so a server that never called publishTourChanges hears of it. A refusal names the runtime tour by its id and points into the submitted tour's JSON, never into the app document. The tours are kept in memory only. Safe to call from any thread.

The bundle getter, the server's texts (ServedBundle::fieldTexts) and the authoriser passed to listFor are called with the registry's lock held, so none of them may call back into the registry or hold a lock around a call into it. The two handlers are called after the lock is released.
