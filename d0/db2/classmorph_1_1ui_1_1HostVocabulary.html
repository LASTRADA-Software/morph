Detailed Description

The client's host vocabulary: what it registered, and whether its documents came over a verified connection.

An application builds one at start-up, registers its commands and values, connects it to the interpreter's Session, and tells it when the connection is verified (spec 5 §13: a verified TLS connection, or loopback with the development flag). Until then a command not marked safeForUntrusted is refused. It lives on the runtime's owner, like the session, and outlives the session it is connected to.
