Detailed Description

The workspace's tabs with no widgets: which screens are open, in which order, which is active, and how they open, close and are recorded (spec 6 §7, §15).

Opening a screen whose identity an open tab already has focuses that tab, a singleton screen has one tab, and a reuseEmpty screen opened with an identity replaces its tab with no identity and no unsaved changes. Closing runs each screen's close protocol (spec 6 §3), one tab at a time, and a walk over several tabs stops at the first veto. next and previous walk the most recently used order. The page each tab shows is the application's: mountPage makes it and mounts the screen there, unmountPage is told when it goes. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its runtime.
