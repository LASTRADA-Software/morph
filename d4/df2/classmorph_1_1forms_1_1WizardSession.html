Detailed Description

The reactive state of one wizard: its steps' sessions, the position, and what each step resolved.

A step is done while its current body is the one whose submission last succeeded: editing a done step makes it not done until that body succeeds. Next moves on from a done step without submitting; from a step that is ready but not done it submits and moves on once that submission succeeds. Back always returns, keeping every step's values. Entering a step applies its prefill from the resolved values as JSON values (programmatic, so it submits nothing by itself); a step left ready and not done by its prefill submits once in automatic mode.

Non-copyable and non-movable: the steps' submitters point into it. Destroy a view of it before the session.
