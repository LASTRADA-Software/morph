Detailed Description

template<typename A, typename... Conds>
struct morph::forms::Or< A, Conds >

Compound condition: at least one of Conds... holds. Nests to any depth, and is usable directly as a top-level formRules entry, exactly like And.

Template Parameters
AAction type every nested condition ranges over.
CondsNested condition node types, at least two.
