How a Badge is drawn.

Enumerator
Filled 

The tone's fill, its border and its colour for the text.

Outline 

The surface's fill, with the tone's colour as border and text.

Quiet 

The surface's fill with the control border and the muted text colour, whatever the tone; the tone colours only an icon. A low-key label, such as the routine priority beside an urgent one.
