virtual void morph::ui::ContainerWidget::moveChild ( Widget & child,
std::size_t index )
pure virtual

Moves child to position index: it is removed, then inserted before the child now at index.

The child is moved, not recreated: it keeps its native state, such as its properties, handlers, focus and selection.

Parameters
childOne of this container's children.
indexThe new position; past the end means last.
