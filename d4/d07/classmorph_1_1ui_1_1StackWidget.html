◆ setWrap()

virtual void morph::ui::StackWidget::setWrap ( bool wrap)
pure virtual

Makes a horizontal stack wrap: its children fill lines in order, and a child that does not fit after the ones already on a line starts the next. The mount calls it once, before the stack's first child, and only for a stack that wraps.

Each shown child has a basis along the row: a content child its natural width, a fixed child its amount, and a stretch child its minimum width, else its natural width; each clamped into the child's bounds and at most the row's width. A line always holds one child; a child whose basis is 0 takes no gap and never starts a line. On a line, the stretch children share the spare width by weight, each stopping at its maximum; a line with none places it by the stack's justify. A line is as tall as its tallest child, the stack's align places a child down its line, and lines are gap apart, the step children are apart along a line. A child wider than the stack sits alone on its line at the stack's width. The stack's natural width is its children's on one line, and its natural height the height of its lines at the width it is given.

Parameters
wrapAlways true: a stack that does not wrap is never told.
