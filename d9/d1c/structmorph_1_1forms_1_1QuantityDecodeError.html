Detailed Description

Thrown when a decoded action has a Quantity field whose engaged value falls outside its unit's declared bounds (UnitTraits<E>::bounds).

Distinct from morph::model::ValidationError: this is a decode-level rejection — a wire payload that violates a physical/unit constraint baked into the field's type, caught before an action's own validate() (a business-rule check) ever runs. See docs/spec/forms/forms.md, "Pre-decode wire validation — `checkQuantityBounds`".
