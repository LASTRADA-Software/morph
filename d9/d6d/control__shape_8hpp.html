Detailed Description

morph::ui::ControlShape and morph::ui::ButtonSize: the outline and the density of a button or a badge. The view tree, the authoring API and the backends share them. Specified in docs/spec/ui/backend_contract.md.

◆ ControlShape

enum class morph::ui::ControlShape : std::uint8_t
strong

The outline of a button or a badge.

Enumerator
Default 

The theme's corners.

Round 

Corners of half the height, and at least as wide as tall: a circle for a short label, a stadium for a longer one.

Square 

A button for a glyph (‹, ⋯): the theme's corners, no least width, little side padding, at least as wide as tall and a strong caption at the body size. A longer caption gives a wider button. Only a button has it; a badge does not.

◆ ButtonSize

enum class morph::ui::ButtonSize : std::uint8_t
strong

How much room a button gives its caption.

Enumerator
Normal 

The control's padding and the body size.

Small 

A compact caption: the least side padding and the small type step, at the theme's button.smallHeight, which may be above or below the control height; without it, the control height.
