Detailed Description

The app explorer, as a host screen (spec 6 §15): a splitter with the list — a search, the catalog's screens, and every model the catalog names with every action of it — and the chosen action's sections: its facts, its fields, the places that use it, and "Try it", the widget the framework builds for it, mounted live.

Built from backend widgets, so every frontend shows it. Its title is "Explorer"; it is never dirty, and closes when asked. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its backend.

Test ids: explorer, explorer_search, explorer_screen_<id>, explorer_model_<type>, explorer_action_<id>, explorer_title, explorer_facts, explorer_fields, explorer_uses, explorer_open_<n>, explorer_tryit, explorer_tryit_status, explorer_schema, and inside "Try it" the generated document's own (tryit, …).
