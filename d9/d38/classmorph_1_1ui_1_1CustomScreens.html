Detailed Description

A server's custom screens: the store, the bundle being served, the authoriser, the bounds and the verdict cache that the model and anything beside it (an assistant that proposes screens) share.

registerCustomScreens makes it and returns it. Every member is safe to call from any thread: the verdict cache is locked, the store is the application's, and the bundle is read through the getter given at registration.
