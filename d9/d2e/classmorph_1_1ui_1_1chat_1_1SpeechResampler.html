Detailed Description

Turns a device's samples, at any rate and channel count, into 16 kHz mono 16-bit, one chunk at a time.

Channels are averaged. Down-sampling averages the input samples one output sample spans, a box filter that attenuates most of what lies above the new Nyquist frequency; up-sampling interpolates between neighbours. Every position is computed in integers from the output sample's index and the samples consumed, never accumulated, so chunks give exactly what one pass would.
