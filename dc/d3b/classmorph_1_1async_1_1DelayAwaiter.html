Detailed Description

The awaiter delay() returns: suspends until a TimeoutScheduler entry fires, or until a stop withdraws it.

Resumes on the executor the awaiting coroutine was running on (core-cpp's current executor), or on the scheduler's thread if there was none. With a stoppable token on the awaiting promise, a stop cancels the scheduler entry – releasing its capture at once – and resumes the coroutine with core::async::OperationCancelled on the same executor, or, with none, inline on the thread that requested the stop. Exactly one of the timer and the stop resumes it.
