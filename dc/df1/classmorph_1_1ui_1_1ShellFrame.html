Detailed Description

The frame around a client's screens.

A row filling the window: the sidebar (surface sidebar, kShellSidebarWidth units wide), holding the application's name and a Navigator, and the main column holding the top bar (surface toolbar) above the page (surface page), where the client places the workspace. The top bar is one line that wraps only when it is out of room: the shown screen's title with the active head's lead (subtitle and badges) under it, and the head's trail (tools and actions, then the principal while it sits in the head) at the trailing edge, dropping to a line of its own where it does not fit. The principal and a sign-out button sit at the sidebar's foot. Signed out, the sidebar and the top bar are hidden and the page is all there is. Compact (setCompact(true)), the sidebar is hidden and the top bar starts with a navigation menu over the navigator's entries, ending with the principal's sign-out entry, so the page has nearly the whole window. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its backend.
