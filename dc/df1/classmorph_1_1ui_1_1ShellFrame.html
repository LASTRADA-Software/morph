Detailed Description

The frame around a client's screens.

A row filling the window: the sidebar (surface sidebar, kShellSidebarWidth units wide), holding the application's name and a Navigator, and the main column holding the top bar (surface toolbar: the shown screen's title, a slot for its active tab's head row, the principal and a sign-out button) above the page (surface page), where the client places the workspace. Signed out, the sidebar and the top bar are hidden and the page is all there is. Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner thread of its backend.
