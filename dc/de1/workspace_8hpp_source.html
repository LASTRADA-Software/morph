60/// @brief The parts of the active screen's head, for a host that lays them out itself.
61struct HeadParts {
62 /// @brief The eyebrow, muted, the line a host draws above the title; hidden while the head has none.

66 /// under one another where they do not.

76/// @brief Tabs over screens: a tab bar over a `WorkspaceModel`, one screen instance per tab, mounted into the tab's
77/// page through `DocumentScreenHost::mountInto`, with the screen's head row above it or, when the placement is
78/// `External`, mounted by the host instead.
79///
80/// The model holds the tab logic: tabs keep the order they were opened in, opening a screen whose identity an open
81/// tab already has focuses that tab, a singleton screen has one tab, and closing runs each screen's close protocol
82/// (spec 6 §3), one tab at a time, a walk over several tabs stopping at the first veto. The bar shows the model's tabs
83/// and takes Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab and Ctrl+W, which documents may not declare. A screen a document navigates to as
84/// a dialog or a drawer is shown over the tabs (`openOverlay`). Neither copyable nor movable; owned by the owner
85/// thread of its runtime.

132 /// @brief Shows a document screen over the tabs, as a dialog (`as: dialog`) or a drawer on the end side
133 /// (`as: drawer`), mounted with `DocumentScreenHost::mountInto`, so the mount adopts the navigation's call.

135 /// It always mounts a new instance: it never focuses an open tab, so a call is never left unclaimed. The
136 /// overlay is titled by the screen's title, and the screen's head actions are its footer's buttons; its badges,
