Detailed Description

A Dispatcher that holds a queued call the server cannot be reached for, and replays it on reconnect.

A call made with CallOptions::queueOffline goes through the wrapped dispatcher. When it fails with backend::DisconnectedError it is held, and the completion the caller has stays pending: the mutation stays pending and its elements stale. Once anything is held, a later queued call joins the back of the queue without being tried, so the server sees the calls in the order they were made. replay re-sends the held calls, oldest first and one at a time, and settles each caller's completion with the replay's reply or failure; a replay that cannot reach the server either stops the replay and stays held for the next one. A call without queueOffline, and an evaluation, are never held: offline, they fail at once.

Single-threaded: every call, and replay, runs on the owner the replies are delivered on. Held calls keep their channel, and with it the model binding, until they settle.
