Detailed Description

The reactive state of one collection: the list, the table source, the actions, the confirm step and the row editor. A controller in the sense of docs/spec/reactive/control.md; it draws nothing.

Non-copyable and non-movable: replies and the editor point into it. Destroy a view of it before the session.

Fires model().actions()[action], on rowKey for a row action. A confirm-guarded action waits in pendingConfirm() instead. While another action is in flight the call does nothing (the actions are one Exclusive mutation, and a view disables its buttons while actionPending()).
