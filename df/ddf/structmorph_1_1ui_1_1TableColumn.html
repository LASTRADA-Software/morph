std::string label
 The header caption.
Sizing width = Sizing::content()
 The column's width request. A Stretch column starts from its content (the larger of its header and its widest shown cell) and its weight shares only the width the content leaves; a table too narrow for the content narrows its wrapping columns, then its shrink columns, last first, then the other stretch columns. A hidden cell widens no column.
TextRole role = TextRole::Normal
 The text style of this column's default cell; Normal renders unchanged. Has no effect on the style of a column's own .cell, or on a Table constructed directly without going through the interpreter. A Mono role also sets the column's width unit: a fixed width counts monospace characters.
Align align = Align::Start
 Where a cell sits across the column: the text of a text cell or a text input cell, and a label-width cell (a badge, a check box, a push button) at its natural width, at the start, centre or end; the header label follows it. A container cell fills the column whatever this says. Stretch reads as Start.
Align valign = Align::Center
 Where a cell sits down its row when the row is taller than the cell: Start at the top, Center, or End at the bottom. Stretch fills the row's height.
bool sortable = false
 Whether a click on the column's header asks to sort by it (Table::onSort).
bool wrap = true
 Whether the column's text cells may break across lines. With false each is drawn on one line and cut at the column's width with an ellipsis, such as a value with its unit in a narrow column.
bool shrink = false
 Whether a Stretch column may be narrower than its header when the table is narrower than its columns need: it then gives up width down to a small floor and cuts its header and cells at its edge (an ellipsis where the backend draws one), where otherwise no column is narrower than its header and the table scrolls sideways instead. A shrink column also gives up its width before the other stretch columns do. It reads as false on a column that does not stretch.
