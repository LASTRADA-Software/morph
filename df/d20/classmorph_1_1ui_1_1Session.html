Detailed Description

What screens of one client session share (spec 5 §5, §12b): the query cache, refreshOn bookkeeping, the principal's permitted actions, the formatting facts and the client's env, session and host values.

It belongs to the runtime's owner, outlives every screen mounted on it, and is cleared by signOut and on a principal change.
