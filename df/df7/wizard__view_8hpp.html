ui::Node morph::forms::wizardView ( WizardSession & session,
WizardViewOptions const & options = {} )
inlinenodiscard

Renders session: the header (title with the step's position, a step indicator), the current step's formView (one per step, mounted while it is current; the values live in the steps' sessions, so leaving a step loses nothing), and Back and Next, enabled by canBack and canNext.

Test ids: wizard_title, wizard_steps, wizard_step_<index> (the current step's form), wizardBack, wizardNext and wizard_last; the step's fields have their form ids.

The nodes bind to the session by pointer: mount the result after the session and destroy the mount before it.

Parameters
sessionThe session.
optionsEvery step's form options, the chrome and the labels.
Returns
The view.
