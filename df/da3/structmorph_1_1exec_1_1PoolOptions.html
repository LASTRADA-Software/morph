Detailed Description

How a ThreadPoolExecutor's idle workers wait for work.

A worker whose queue is empty may spin on an atomic for a short time before it sleeps on the condition variable. A task posted during the spin is taken with no system call on either side: the poster does not notify, and the worker never slept.

◆ spinBeforeSleep

std::chrono::microseconds morph::exec::PoolOptions::spinBeforeSleep {25}

How long one worker spins after the queue empties, before it sleeps. Zero disables spinning: a worker sleeps the moment it finds nothing to do, which is the right choice for a battery-sensitive application. A worker burns at most this much CPU after each task that leaves the queue empty, and none while the pool is idle.

◆ maxSpinners

std::size_t morph::exec::PoolOptions::maxSpinners = 1

How many workers may spin at once. The others sleep at once, so a pool of many workers costs one spinning core, not all of them. Zero also disables spinning.
