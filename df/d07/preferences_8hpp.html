Detailed Description

Per-person storage of customization choices (spec 5 §12b, spec 6 §8): the IPreferencesStore seam, the framework's in-memory store, and the server's preferences model, which keys every entry by the person the verified session names.

inline

Registers the preferences model and its three actions, with a factory that gives every instance store.

Nothing registers it at static initialisation: a server that offers preferences calls this once, before it serves, with the dispatcher and registry its RemoteServer uses. A deployment's authoriser then admits morph.preferences and its actions load, set and remove for every signed-in principal; the model itself keeps each person to their own entries.
