Detailed Description

The exact arithmetic of a chart's axes: the padded range of its values and the ticks that label it.

Specified in docs/spec/ui/interpreter.md, "Charts". Everything here is math::Rational; a chart converts to double only once its range and ticks are known, so no label is derived from a rounded position.

◆ chartRange()

std::optional< ChartRange > morph::ui::chartRange ( std::span< math::Rational const > values,
std::uint32_t places )
inlinenodiscard

The axis that holds every value, widened by 5 % of their span at each end so the outermost value is inside the plot rather than on its edge.

A span of zero is widened by one unit in the last of places either side; no values at all give 0 to 1.

Parameters
valuesThe values the axis must hold.
placesThe places a value is shown at, which size the widening of a zero span; at most 18.
Returns
The range, or empty when the arithmetic overflows.

inlinenodiscard

The ticks of a value axis: the multiples, inside range, of the largest step of 1, 2 or 5 × 10ᵏ that puts three or more there.

Three to six ticks, except where a step of 5 × 10ᵏ would put two and the next, 2 × 10ᵏ, puts seven or eight.

Parameters
rangeThe axis, its high end above its low end.
Returns
The ticks, or empty when the range is too narrow for a step of 10⁻¹⁸ or the arithmetic overflows.
