Detailed Description

Broader than EmptyCapableField: also covers a plain std::optional<T> member (e.g. std::optional<std::string> email), which does not satisfy EmptyCapableField — it exposes has_value(), not hasValue() (see forms.md's allRequiredEngaged "two exclusions" note). The cross-field rule vocabulary's engagement checks (engaged, notEngaged, requiredWhen, and the membership rules added in a later task) accept either kind of field, since the planned spec's own worked example ranges an exactlyOneOf over two plain std::optional<std::string> fields.
