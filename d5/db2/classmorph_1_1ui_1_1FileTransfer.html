Detailed Description

The client of the file side channel (files/1, spec 6 §18): uploads and downloads through ui-file, one chunk of at most kMaxFileChunkBytes in flight at a time, reporting progress after each.

What it moves is bytes: reading the file the user picked is the platform's, and an application's InterpreterHost::upload reads it and hands the bytes here.
