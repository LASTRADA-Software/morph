Detailed Description

The headless controller of custom screens over one workspace: what each tab holds and the commands over it, with no view of its own.

It keeps, per tab, the draft's text, a generation that changes with every draft mounted or ended, whether a save is in flight and the last refusal of a save. Its commands mount a checked edit as a tab's draft, save it as a new screen or over the person's own, discard it, open a screen in a tab's place, and remove a screen; an answer acts only on the draft it was asked about, and one that finds the tab holding another draft, or none, or closed, leaves the tab as it is and is told as an outcome. Outcomes and refusals are data (CustomScreenOutcome, CustomScreenCheckResult): wording them, in whatever language, is the view's. Its state is reactive, so a view's bindings follow it. Everything runs on the runtime's owner. Neither copyable nor movable.
