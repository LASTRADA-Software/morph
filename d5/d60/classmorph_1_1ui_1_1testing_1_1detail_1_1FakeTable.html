Detailed Description

A fake TableWidget: properties columns, selectionMode, selection, and rowKey on a row.

selection shows the rows the widget marks: the requested keys, in the order requested, that one of its current rows has. The requested keys are kept, so a row that arrives with one of them is marked and a row that goes is unmarked; that re-marking is the widget's own doing and is not logged.
