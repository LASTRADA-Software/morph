Detailed Description

One x-rules entry, or a condition inside one, and its translation into expr/1.

The 16 kinds the emitters write are read; any other kind parses and is Unknown, so an older client never refuses a newer server's form. A rule's operands are numbered f0, f1, … in its translation, and each operand reads as two names: fN.engaged (true, false, or null when its draft does not encode) and fN.value (the encoded value, null unless it is valid). The two are needed because a blank operand and an invalid one are both "no value" to the expression language, and the rules tell them apart: a comparison with a blank operand holds vacuously, one with an invalid operand is Unknown.
