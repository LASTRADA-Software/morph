Detailed Description

The custom documents a client has fetched, by id: what DocumentScreenHostOptions::documents answers for a custom: id.

The documents lookup is synchronous, so whatever opens a custom screen (a navigator entry, Open as, a restored tab) has its document fetched into the cache first. A list that shows a kept screen with another hash, or a whole list that no longer shows it, drops it, so the next open fetches the stored document again.
