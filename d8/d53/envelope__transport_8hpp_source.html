17/// @brief What a wire transport offers beside `IBackend`: one request, one reply, matched by `callId`.
18///
19/// `SocketBackend` and `QtWebSocketBackend` implement it. A backend without a wire, such as `LocalBackend`, has no
20/// peer and does not.

29 /// @brief Sends one request envelope and settles with its reply.
30 ///
31 /// The envelope travels with the session it carries: the transport assigns its `callId` and stamps nothing else,
32 /// because a protocol that passes its session explicitly (`ui::UiClient::open`) must not have it replaced by the
33 /// transport's default. The reply resolves the `Completion` whether it is `ok` or `err`, since reading an `err`
34 /// is the caller's protocol; only a failure to deliver the request or its reply rejects it. A request made while
35 /// the connection is down is held and sent at the next connect; `cancelPending`, a drop and the transport's
36 /// destruction reject what is pending or held.
37 /// @param envelope The request. Its `callId` is replaced.
38 /// @param cbExec Executor the answer is delivered on. Borrowed: it must outlive the returned `Completion`.
39 /// @return A `Completion` resolved with the reply envelope, or rejected with the transport's failure
40 /// (`DisconnectedError` for a dropped or closed connection).
41 virtual ::morph::async::Completion<::morph::wire::Envelope> request(::morph::wire::Envelope envelope,
42 ::morph::exec::IExecutor& cbExec) = 0;
43
44protected:
45 ~IEnvelopeTransport() = default;
46};
