Detailed Description

The server's custom screens model: the caller's screens, through the application's store, checked.

The person is the principal of the verified session, read once at the start of each action, before anything is awaited. No request names a person, and one that tries is ignored. With no verified principal, list answers nothing and every other action is refused.
