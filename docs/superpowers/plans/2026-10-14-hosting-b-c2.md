## What planning found

1. **No layer carries out `focus` (#937).** The view tree moves focus only at mount (`autofocus`); the interpreter
   forwards `{"focus": id}` to `InterpreterHost::focus`, whose default refuses, and `DocumentScreenHost` forwards it
   to the application. The fix is a focus request through the mounted tree by the element's id, which
   `Widget::focus()` carries out in every backend; the interpreter then does `focus` itself, and the hook goes.
2. **`app.name` reads nothing today.** Spec 6 §5's app document is parsed and validated, but nothing mounts it:
   `{"ref": "app.x"}` evaluates to `null` and `{"set": "app.x"}` goes to `InterpreterHost::setAppValue`.
3. **A backend switch is synchronous and runs no screen's `onBackendChange`.** `InterpreterHost::switchBackend`
   answers a bool and `Session::backendChanged` refetches at once; spec 6 §9 wants an asynchronous commit or
   failure, `onBackendChange` before the refetch, and `env.backend` published.
4. **Accessibility and test identity are already on Qt Quick.** `MorphWidget` binds `Accessible.name` and
   `Accessible.role`, and `testId` is the item's `objectName` (`test_qt_quick_widgets.cpp`). What spec 6 §6 adds —
   a click handler's default `button` role, a read-only value named `caption: value`, `Accessible.id` — is (c2)'s.

## Design

### Focus by element id (#937, its own commit)

- `Common::focusId`: the name a focus request finds the widget by; the interpreter sets it from the element's `id`.
- The mounter keeps a multimap of focus name to widget, each entry owned by the widget's scope after the widget,
  so content that unmounts (a Switch case, a page, a row) leaves the map before its widget is destroyed.
  `Mounted::focus(name)` focuses the first mounted, and answers whether one was found.
- `{"focus": id}` is carried out by the screen's mounted tree; an element that is not mounted is reported and fails
  the command. `InterpreterHost::focus` is removed: nothing asks it any more.

### The app root scope (§5)

- `Interpreter::mountApp(doc)` mounts the app document as an `AppRoot`: its scope, state, lets, queries and
  mutations, once per session; refused for a document whose id is not `app` and for a second root.
- `Session::appScope()`: `{"ref": "app.name"}` reads the name in the root's scope from any screen, reactively;
  `{"set": "app.name"}` writes the root's state (a name that is not state is reported). Without a root, `set` still
  goes to the host's `setAppValue`.
- Destroying the root runs its `onUnmount` and takes its scope off the session. A host destroys its screens first,
  then the root, then clears the session, as sign-out requires.

### The backend switch (§9)

- `InterpreterHost::switchBackend(name, done)`: answers whether the backend is listed, and calls `done` once when
  the switch committed or failed.
- On commit, `Session::backendChanged(backend)` runs, in one batch, every mounted screen's and the app root's
  `onBackendChange`, then marks every entry stale and refetches the mounted queries, and sets `env.backend`. A
  failure reports `backendSwitchFailed: reason` and fails the command, so its `onError` path runs and nothing is
  refetched.

## Not in this batch, and why

- **Host components from documents** need the view tree's `Custom` node to carry props and events, and the
  interpreter to choose the component or the fallback at mount; that is a view-tree change of its own.
- **The Qt Quick renderer as a `ScreenHost`** and the remaining accessibility defaults belong with it.
- **Native sign-in and the sign-out order** are the host's: the pieces (`AppRoot`, `Session::clear`) are here.
