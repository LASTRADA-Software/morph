Detailed Description

The client side of the UI service (spec 5 §12, "Content addressing"): UiClient runs ui-hello, ui-catalog and ui-fetch over a transport the caller supplies, verifies every file against the manifest and the manifest against the ui-hello digest, and keeps what it verified in a VerifiedCache.
