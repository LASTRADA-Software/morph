Detailed Description

A window that shows the focused screen's mounted nodes with their evaluated properties, its queries and mutations with a refetch button each, its command log and the reactive counters.

It reads the screen through ui::Inspector each time it refreshes, twice a second while it is shown, and keeps nothing of it in between, so a screen that closes or is remounted leaves nothing behind. Its refetch button runs the document's refetch command, as a user's event would. Use it on the GUI thread.
