Detailed Description

The app document mounted as the root scope every screen of the session shares (spec 6 §5).

Its state, lets, queries and mutations live once for the session. A screen reads them as {"ref": "app.name"}, a binding on one updates, and {"set": "app.name", …} writes the app document's state. A query of the app document runs once however many screens read it. Destroying the root runs its onUnmount, takes its scope off the session and releases its model instances; a host does that before it clears the session on sign-out.
