std::function< void(std::expected< void, std::string >)> done )
inlinevirtual

Switches to a backend the application's source lists (AppSource::switchBackend, spec 6 §9).

The switch is asynchronous: the host calls done once, on the runtime's owner, when it committed or failed. A failed switch leaves the old backend in place.

Parameters
nameThe backend.
doneCalled once with nothing when the switch committed, or with why it failed. Not called when the backend is not listed.
Returns
Whether it is listed.
