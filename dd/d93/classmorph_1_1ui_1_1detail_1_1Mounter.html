Detailed Description

Builds widgets for nodes and wires their props and callbacks.

One per Mounted. It must outlive every scope it mounted into: a Switch, Tabs, Dialog, ForEach or Table binding calls back into it to mount content later.

Mounts node under parent; scope owns its widgets and bindings.

A node's widget is adopted first, then its bindings, then its children, so destroying the scope destroys bindings before their widget and children before their parent.
