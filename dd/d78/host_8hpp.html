◆ CloseGuard

using morph::ui::CloseGuard = std::function<void(ScreenHandle& screen, std::function<void(CloseChoice choice)> choose)>

What decides a close the document does not decide itself: a dirty screen that declares no onClose.

It asks the user, typically with a dialog, and calls choose once. It offers save and close only when screen.canSave(); Save on a screen that cannot save keeps it open. The handle carries the choice out, so choose may be called after the handle is gone, and then does nothing.
