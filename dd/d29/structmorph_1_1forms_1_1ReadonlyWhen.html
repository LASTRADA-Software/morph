Detailed Description

template<typename V, typename A, typename Cond>
struct morph::forms::ReadonlyWhen< V, A, Cond >

Presentation rule: the listed field is editable only while Cond does not hold (the field is read-only while when holds). Never gates allRulesSatisfied, exactly like VisibleWhen.
