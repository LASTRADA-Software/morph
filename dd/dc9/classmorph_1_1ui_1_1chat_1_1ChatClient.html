Detailed Description

Sends what a person says, and keeps what to show for each conversation.

Events are hints and the server's TurnState is the record: the client reads it when a turn starts, when an event says one ended, and on resync, and what it reads replaces what events built. What it shows lasts the session, and only for display. The server keeps the conversation itself.

Everything runs on the owner: every call, and the exchange's replies. A turn belongs to the conversation it started in, so switching conversations does not move it. Neither copyable nor movable.
