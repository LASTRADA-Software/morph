Detailed Description

Opens an application's bundle through the UI service, verifying everything it fetches.

open runs ui-hello, refuses a bundle that needs a vocabulary the client does not speak, takes the manifest from the cache or fetches it by the digest and verifies it, runs ui-catalog, and fetches the files the principal may use that the cache does not hold, all at once, verifying each. A file that does not hash to its manifest entry, or a manifest that does not hash to the digest, refuses the whole bundle (Unverified). An unchanged manifest fetches nothing.
