Names a floating-point reading.

Not noexcept: unlike Base::fromDouble, this wraps the result in a NamedQuantity, whose construction calls named() — and with MORPH_QUANTITY_PROVENANCE enabled named() allocates an ASTNode (std::make_shared), which can throw std::bad_alloc.
