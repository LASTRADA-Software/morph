std::string_view label )

Makes the badge removable: it draws a remove control after its text, drawn as the framework icon close, and takes the focus. Delete, Backspace, Enter or Space on it, or a click on its control, calls onRemove. The mount calls it only for a badge with a remove handler, at mount and whenever the label changes; a badge it is never called on takes no focus.
