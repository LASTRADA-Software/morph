Detailed Description

template<typename E>
struct morph::units::UnitRelation< E >

One exact within-dimension conversion ratio between two peer units.

1 unit of from equals fromTo units of to. fromTo must be strictly positive; from and to must differ. Declared in UnitTraits<E>::relations and used in both directions (the reverse multiplies by the reciprocal).
