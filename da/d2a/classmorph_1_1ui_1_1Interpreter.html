inlinenodiscard

Mounts a screen.

The document has loaded (loadDocument); what mounting adds is what only the opener knows: the params, and the models a {"from": alias} instance borrows. A param that is missing and not optional, a param of the wrong type, a borrowed model the opener did not lend and a document without a view are refused.

Parameters
docThe document.
paramsThe params, by name; names the document does not declare are ignored.
backendThe backend to mount on. Borrowed: it outlives the screen.
optionsWhere to mount, and the models lent.
Returns
The screen, or the refusal.
