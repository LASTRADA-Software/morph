Detailed Description

A mounted screen: its scopes, its view mounted through Mounted, and what its host asks of it.

Neither copyable nor movable. It belongs to the runtime's owner; the runtime, the session, the dispatcher and the backend outlive it. Destroying it runs onUnmount, unmounts the view and releases every scope.
